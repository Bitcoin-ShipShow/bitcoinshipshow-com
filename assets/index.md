05 · The look

## Paper-cut collage meets vintage woodcut 🏴‍☠️

Paste this into any image tool to get the house style. The E01 art below was made with it.

```
Hybrid paper-cut collage meets vintage woodcut poster. Layered cut-paper
shapes with visible torn edges, bold black woodcut outlines, limited ink
palette (deep navy, cream, warm gold, optional warm red), paper grain,
mid-century print feel. Minimal detail, generous empty space.
No text, no logo, no watermark.
```

### Do ✓

- use the palette, Pirata One, sharp corners, hard 10px offset shadows
- keep it flat and layered — cut paper, not 3D
- leave generous empty space so the title can breathe
- set the event type yourself; generated text always misspells a guest

### Don't ✕

- neon, gradients, glassmorphism, rounded corners
- photoreal textures, glossy highlights, blurred drop shadows
- geometric sans or system fonts in body copy
- recolour or rotate the boat, or lock extra words into the logo

06 · Naming

## How to write it

- **Bitcoin Shipshow** two words, both capitalised — never "BitcoinShipshow" or "Bitcoin Ship Show"
- **Shipshow** one word when short, e.g. "the Shipshow"
- **Episodes** E01, E02, E03 … subtitle in quotes: E01 · Pirates' Community Call
- **Schedule** monthly, first Tuesday, ~90 min on air, ~30 min off record
- **Times** 13:00 ET / 19:00 CET, never UTC
- **Hashtag** #bitcoinshipshow secondary: #shipshow
- **URL** [bitcoinship.show](https://bitcoinship.show) · watch [/live](https://bitcoinship.show/live/)
- **X** [@bitcoinshipshow](https://x.com/bitcoinshipshow)

07 · Copy

## Text you can reuse 📋

### One line

```
Bitcoin Shipshow — a monthly live show where builders demo what they are actually shipping.
```

### Short paragraph

```
Bitcoin Shipshow is a monthly live show where builders demo what they are
actually shipping in Bitcoin, Lightning and Nostr. Live demos, not pitches.
Give your email and the room link lands in your inbox.
```

### Long paragraph

```
Bitcoin Shipshow is a monthly live show where builders demo what they are
actually shipping in Bitcoin, Lightning and Nostr — early projects, a
well-known project showing a fresh release, and a short slot for something
still too early to demo. Live demos, not pitches. The last half hour is off
the record: no recording, no clips, just the room.
```

### Post-ready (X / LinkedIn) 📱

```
E01 is Tue 6 Oct — 13:00 ET / 19:00 CET.
Pakstr, IndeeHub, Sidecar and Angor demo what they shipped.
Live demos, not pitches. bitcoinship.show #bitcoinshipshow 🏴‍☠️
```
