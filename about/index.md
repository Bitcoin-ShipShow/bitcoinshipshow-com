## The show

- 3–5live project demos per expedition
- ~90min public show, streamed & published
- ~30min private off-record room
- 3years of community-call legacy

Bitcoin Shipshow is an **online Bitcoin meetup** that behaves like
a demo show. It is about building and creating — not price, not punditry. Each expedition
mixes early, unknown builders with one well-known project showing a **fresh release**,
plus a short slot for someone whose thing is still too early to demo properly.

Every expedition becomes quality video content: full episodes and clips,
published across platforms, so the show doubles as a rolling archive of what just shipped in
Bitcoin, Lightning and Nostr. The last half hour is the **community call** proper —
off the record, no recording, no clips, just the room.

## Fresh releases, every month

Every expedition puts at least one well-known project on stage with a
**fresh release**, and the rest of the line-up is whatever just shipped: a new
wallet build, a new Nostr client, a signer that finally works, a Bitcoin film that just got
finished, a hub opening its circular economy. New Bitcoin projects and new versions of
familiar ones get the same slot and the same time.

If it runs, it is ready. Alpha and beta are welcome, and so is a project nobody
has heard of yet — one slot every month is deliberately kept for exactly that. What we do not
take is slideware: the demo is the real thing running on the builder's screen, in front of a
room that asks questions.

- A new app, or a first public demo
- A new version of something people already use
- Protocol, tooling or open-source releases
- Hardware, signers & new revisions
- Bitcoin films, music & art
- A meetup, hub or circular economy launching

Catching up is easy: every release that gets demoed is published afterwards as
an episode or a clip on
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BitcoinShipshow)
and announced on Nostr and X under
[#bitcoinshipshow](https://x.com/bitcoinshipshow).

## Questions, answered

The short version of everything above — what Bitcoin Shipshow is, when it runs,
who it is for and how to get your project on stage.

### What is Bitcoin Shipshow?

Bitcoin Shipshow is a monthly online Bitcoin meetup with a live demo show in the middle of it — part TV show, part community call. Every first Tuesday, builders from the Bitcoin, Lightning and Nostr ecosystems demo what they have actually shipped. Live demos, not pitches, and no price talk.

### Is Bitcoin Shipshow a Bitcoin meetup I can join online?

Yes. It is an online-only Bitcoin meetup: no city, no venue, no door fee, no minimum. You sign up with an email, the invitation and the room link arrive by email, and you join from wherever you are — one person on a laptop counts.

### How is it different from a local Bitcoin meetup?

Same instinct, no geography. A local meetup is whoever can get to the room; this is whoever can open a browser, so the room mixes builders from several continents in one call. Local meetups and hubs are welcome to bring their members along, and a replay makes an easy programme for a night you cannot fill.

### When is the next Bitcoin Shipshow?

The first Tuesday of every month, 13:00 ET / 19:00 CET. The same slot every month, for about ninety minutes on the stream and half an hour more with the room open off the record. The countdown on the live page converts the start time to your own clock.

### How is it different from a Bitcoin conference or a Bitcoin podcast?

There is no stage, no ticket, no booth and no slideware — a working demo on camera, then questions from the room. It is live and interactive rather than pre-recorded, and it is free. Every expedition is published afterwards as full episodes and clips, so the show also works as a rolling archive of fresh Bitcoin releases.

### Do you run a Bitcoin demo day or a pitch night?

No. A demo day usually means decks, judging and investors in the room; this is the opposite. Three to five working demos, no judging panel, no pitch deck, no funding round attached. If someone wants feedback, they get it from the people actually building alongside them.

### How do I demo my project on the show?

Leave your email on the front page and say what you are building when you reply, or just turn up to a show and introduce yourself in the room. We book three to five demos per expedition. Working demos only: if it runs, even roughly, it is ready.

### Do I need a finished or well-known product to demo?

No. Early and unknown is exactly what we want. Each episode deliberately mixes first-time builders with one well-known project showing a fresh release, and there is a short slot for someone whose thing is still too early to demo properly.

### Where do I find fresh Bitcoin releases every month?

Here, and it is the whole point of the show. Every expedition puts at least one fresh release on stage alongside work nobody has seen yet, and each episode is published afterwards on YouTube and announced on Nostr and X. Follow #bitcoinshipshow, or leave your email and the invite finds you on the day.

### What kind of projects get on stage?

Anything interesting being built on Bitcoin, Lightning or Nostr: wallets, hubs, signers, Nostr clients and relays, music and media apps, Bitcoin films and art, education projects, meetups and circular economies, dev tools and open source. Beta and alpha are welcome.

### Is Bitcoin Shipshow free to watch?

Yes. Watching is free, demoing is free and no sponsorship is required to get a slot. The show runs on value-for-value: tips and zaps on the stream go to the people building, and hosts do not take a cut of them.

### Who runs Bitcoin Shipshow, and can I sponsor it?

It is independent and community-run: the hosts curate every slot and there is no media company behind it. A handful of aligned partners support each season, and one demo slot every month is reserved for a project with no partner behind it — so a sponsor can never buy the whole room. The partner one-pager is linked on this page.

### Where can I watch past episodes?

Full episodes and clips are published on our YouTube channel and announced on Nostr and X, so the show doubles as an archive of fresh releases you can catch up on. Episode one is already up: Pakstr, IndeeHub, Sidecar and Angor.

### What is the Pirates' Community Call?

It is our tagline for what the show is: a community call that grew out of the Bitcoin community calls the hosts ran for three years, with the pirate flavour of a boat folded out of a newspaper. The public stream ends after about ninety minutes and the room stays open, off the record, for the real talk. Say pirate around here and this is what we mean — no skulls, no menace, just the crew.
